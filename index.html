Welcome to the TCG club! We are a group of students who love trading card games and want to share our passion with others. Whether you're a seasoned player or just starting out, we welcome everyone to join us for fun, learning, and friendly competition. Come meet new friends, learn new strategies, and enjoy the world of trading card games!

Our teachers who play and can help out!

Mr. Smith

I thrive on incremental advantage, synergy, and engine-building rather than overwhelming my opponents with raw power or quick combos. My blink-focused Atraxa deck reflects how much I enjoy reusing ETB effects, getting maximum value from my resources, and turning individual cards into powerful interactions.

Mr. Beck

I am a player with patience and a bringer of inevitable doom. Every enchantment on the board is a brick in a wall—individually modest, but collectively suffocating. I'm not looking for a single explosive turn. I'm taking my opponents down through relentless value and attrition while their permanents quietly accumulate advantage in the background. !

Mr. Huynh

I like to overwhelm the board with tokens while using Goad to force my opponents into fighting each other. My strategy is all about creating chaos, building up an army, and turning my opponents’ creatures against each other while I capitalize on the disorder.
